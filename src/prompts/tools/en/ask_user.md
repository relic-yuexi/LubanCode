## description

When a task hinges on a user choice that would change the direction of the implementation, ask the user with multiple-choice questions. You may ask 1 to 4 questions at a time, each with 2 to 4 options; the interface appends a "write your own" entry automatically. Do not use it for details you can find out yourself.

## param.questions

Array of full questions (1 to 4)

## param.questions.header

Short header for the question; preferably no more than 12 characters

## param.questions.question

The full question

## param.questions.options

Array of options (2 to 4 per question)

## param.questions.options.label

Short label of the option

## param.questions.options.description

Description of the option (optional)

## param.questions.multi_select

Whether multiple selections are allowed; default false
